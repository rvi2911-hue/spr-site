Directional forecast

2027–2031

Not an attempt to predict every event. The aim is to identify the directions in which processes are most likely to develop over the next 1–5 years.

01

AI networks and agents

A shift from individual AI tools toward connected systems in which several agents perform different parts of one task.

02

Bioengineering

The convergence of biology, computing, materials and engineering creates conditions for new applications.

03

Autonomous transport

After specialized use, autonomous systems may expand into freight logistics, transport and other physical processes.

04

Investment flows

As geopolitical uncertainty decreases, capital may seek new production and consumer configurations, including India.

By year

2027

AI agents in smartphones and further reduction of human effort in digital interaction.

2028

A possible transition point for global connections and investment flows.

2029

AI inverse design from specified parameters with physical samples for validation against reality.

2030

Networks of AI agents as the next level of digital process connectivity.

2031

Expansion of AI into transport and physical logistics.

How to read the forecast

This is a directional forecast: the transition direction matters more than an exact date for an individual event. It can be checked and updated as new data and connections appear.
