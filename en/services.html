For researchers, strategists and business

What can be commissioned

Research can focus on a specific process, problem, technology, market or evolving system — from its current state to the probable next state and direction of development.

01

Complex process analysis

Current state, hidden connections, feedback loops, turning points and factors determining further movement.

02

Directional forecast

Identification of the most probable directions of process development over a horizon of 1 to 15 years, distinguishing near-term and longer-term transitions.

03

Hypothesis testing

Independent research of an idea, technology, market or other process, including supporting and contradictory connections.

04

Hidden-connection research

Several processes are treated as one system to identify connections not visible when objects are analyzed separately.

05

Technology or market research

Assessment of the next stage of a process: what may emerge, what conditions are required and what connections may accelerate or slow the transition.

06

Comprehensive research

Full contour: initial state → processes and connections → changes → transition → new state → direction of further development.

Working format

Research horizon: 1–15 years. The specific horizon depends on the speed of the process: shorter for rapidly changing processes, longer for fundamental and slow processes.

The result is a structured analysis and development vector, not an attempt to predict every individual event.

Who may benefit

Research groups, strategists, technology companies, investors, market analysts and anyone needing independent research into an evolving process.

Result

A structured analysis of the process, key connections and transitions, major uncertainties and a development vector.
