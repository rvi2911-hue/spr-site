Public research

Process of humanity
1960–2026

How the process of humanity changed over 66 years through population, economy, physical logistics, technology and changing connections between them.

The aim is not to list events, but to view the period as one process: initial state → development → changes in rate → turning points → new state.

1960–1970

Transition to mass electronics, computing, containerization and a new industrial base.

1970–2000

Computing, communications, global logistics and the Internet form a new level of connectivity.

2000–2026

Smartphones, cloud computing, AI and digital networks move more processes into a connected digital environment.

What is examined

Population

Population dynamics as a basic parameter of the process of humanity.

Economy

Changes in global GDP and development rates over a long time horizon.

Technology

Not only invention dates, but stages of mass implementation.

Main result

A statistical first pass shows individual changes. A wider process contour makes it possible to view them as connected transitions: demography, economy, technology and physical flows change system connectivity simultaneously, but with different time lags.

This research is the basis for the separate 2027–2031 development forecast.
