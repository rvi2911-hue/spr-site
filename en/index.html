Research into complex processes

Spiral Process Reality does not examine isolated objects, but the process as a whole: the initial state, interactions, changes in connectivity, accumulation of changes, transition and the new state.

Process

Not only what exists now, but the path that brought the system to this state.

Connections

A change in system connectivity can be more important than a change in individual elements.

Transition

Accumulation of changes leads to a transition and the formation of a new state.

Initial state→Processes and connections→Feedback loops→Change in connectivity→Accumulation→Transition→New state

Development forecast 2027–2031

The aim is not to predict every individual event. The goal is to identify the directions in which the process is most likely to develop over the next 1–5 years.

AI networks and AI agents

Transition from individual AI tools to connected systems and networks.

Controlled long-duration fusion reaction

According to the Spiral Process Reality forecast, a stable, controlled long-duration fusion process will not be achieved until the physical nature of gravity is understood and artificial gravity can be created. A key condition will be controlled gravitational compression of plasma.

Bioengineering

The convergence of biology, computing, materials and engineering technologies.

Autonomous transport

Expansion of autonomous systems beyond specialized applications.

2028 — end of the period of major confrontations

According to the Spiral Process Reality forecast, the war in Ukraine and the confrontation between Iran and the United States are expected to end by 2028.

This would become a transition point: released resources, changes in investment flows and restored international connections would form a new configuration of global development.

From this point, the next stage of the process begins, considered in the forecast through 2035.

Main forecast after 2035

The main change for humanity will be a transition from an object-based view of reality to a process-based one.

After 2035, process-based thinking will gradually become a more important way of understanding reality: attention will shift from individual objects to processes, connections, interactions and changes in the system as a whole.

Model ≠ reality

Spiral Process Reality is not a claim that reality is structured exactly this way. It is an observation tool — a framework, GPS and microscope that can reduce research effort and reveal the complete contour of a process.

What can be commissioned

Research and forecasting of complex processes

A process, problem or evolving system can be submitted for research — from its current state to the probable next state and direction of development.

1. Analysis of a complex process

Analysis of the current state, hidden connections, feedback loops, turning points and factors determining further development.

2. Directional forecast

Identification of the most probable directions of process development over a horizon of 1 to 15 years, distinguishing near-term and longer-term transitions.

3. Hypothesis testing

Independent examination of a proposed idea, technology, market or other process, including supporting and contradictory connections.

4. Search for hidden connections

Treating several processes as one system to identify connections that are not visible when individual objects are analyzed separately.

5. Research into a future technology or market

Assessment of what next stage may emerge from an ongoing process and what conditions are required for its realization.

6. Comprehensive research

The full contour: initial state → processes and connections → changes → transition → new state → forecast of further development.

Working format

Research horizon: 1–5 years. The specific horizon depends on the speed of the process: shorter for rapidly changing processes, longer for fundamental and slow processes.

The result is a structured analysis and development vector, not an attempt to predict every individual event.
